CrossingTime build 28: listen

Everything here is what the app itself will play. Press a button to hear it. Nothing is sent anywhere; the page only plays files from this folder.

1. Right, wrong and your own sound

"Classic" is the plain tone used up to build 27. "Bright" (the new default) and "Warm" are the two new styles. In each new style, the wrong sound A opens low and the right sound opens higher, so they differ from the first note.

SoundClassic (old)Bright (new default)Warm (new)

2. The warning-time sound (the steady sound that is timed)

The old one was two plain tones together, which is why it sounded like a telephone dial tone. The new one is a single note with its own overtones, steady from start to end. Each button plays 3 seconds.

Pitch settingClassic (old)New

3. A whole replay, old and new

A 4-second warning sound, your own sound at 3.2 seconds, and the result at 4 seconds.

4. The voice

"Natural" is the new recorded voice (the same voice as the SOMA demo video). "This computer's voice" is the built-in voice of the device, which is what the app used until now; on an iPhone it is the robotic one.

What the app saysNatural (new)This computer's voice

A sentence with a number or a name in it (for example "You were 0.4 seconds early") cannot be recorded ahead of time, so the app still says those with the phone's voice.
